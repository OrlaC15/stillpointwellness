About Me

A space held with warmth and without judgement

I am an integrative psychotherapist based in the heart of Wicklow, Ireland. My practice draws on a range of therapeutic modalities — bringing together body-based, relational, and narrative approaches — to meet you where you are.

Whether you are facing burnout, grief, identity shifts, or simply a quiet sense that something needs to change, I offer a grounded, caring presence to help you find your way forward.

My Approach

Grounded in relationship, led by your needs

No two people are the same, and no single method fits every story. My integrative approach means I draw from multiple frameworks, always guided by what is most helpful for you.

  • Person-centred relational foundations
  • Somatic and body-aware practices
  • Attachment and developmental perspectives
  • Mindfulness and present-moment awareness
  • Narrative and meaning-making approaches
